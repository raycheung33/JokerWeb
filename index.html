CHAT BEFORE YOU'RE FLUENT

Language learning without expensive tutors, endless grammar drills, or waiting until you are "fluent enough" to speak. BlowWater turns real-time conversations with real people into your primary study material from day one.

REAL-TIME DUAL-LANGUAGE CHAT

Write in your native language. Your message sends in the language you are learning, and their reply arrives translated back to you. Both versions stay on screen so you always retain full context while building real conversational confidence.

CONTEXT-BASED LEARNING

  • Word Translation: Tap any word in a message to see its meaning, hear pronunciation, and save it to your deck.
  • Conversational Memory: Saved words remember the exact sentence they came from—no random, out-of-context word lists.
  • Full Translation: Long-press any complete message for an instant full translation.

DAILY QUIZZES

  • Turn your past conversations into interactive multiple-choice quizzes.
  • Smart review intervals move correct answers further out and bring difficult words back sooner.
  • Your next daily match stays blurred until you complete your quick review.

PRONUNCIATION & SPEAKING FEEDBACK

Read messages aloud to get pinpoint accuracy feedback. BlowWater highlights the specific sounds or words you missed instead of giving a generic score out of ten. Tap to hear audio playback anytime to practice mimicking real speech.
